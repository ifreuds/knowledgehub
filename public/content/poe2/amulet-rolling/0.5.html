Amulet Rolling — Endgame Profit Craft

Turn cheap high-item-level amulets into jackpot mods with pure chaos spam. Target the pricey late-game bases (the Absent Amulet from Breach nets the most divine/hour) — but any amulet works. It's genuinely profitable and simple; the only hard part is that it's tiring on the eyes.

The loop

01

Buy a cheap ilvl 75+ amulet base and get it to rare with a single mod.

02

Chaos-spam it — each orb rerolls one random mod from the pool.

03

Stop the instant you hit a jackpot: T1 Spirit or a +3 to a skill type.

04

Sell in Divine. The hit value dwarfs the chaos you spent → net-positive EV.

Why it's tiring, not hard: you must watch every single roll and never chaos over a mod that's already worth selling. That vigilance is the whole skill — the maths below is always in your favour.

The ilvl-75 myth. Sellers charge a premium for ilvl-75 bases because the smaller mod pool gives a slightly better jackpot chance. But the pool only grows from 163,800 (ilvl 75) to 173,650 (ilvl 82) — about 6% more rolls. Unless the ilvl-75 surcharge is smaller than the “extra rolls” cost shown above, just buy a cheap high-ilvl base.

Weights are the real 0.5 amulet pool (verified against Craft of Exile / poe2db). T1 Spirit = weight 400 (+47–50), each +3 skill = weight 100. Chance = mod weight ÷ total pool. Phase 2 will add an outcome logger so you can record real spends and sales.
