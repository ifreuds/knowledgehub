Skip the ilvl-75 taxQUICK READ

Yet sellers price ilvl-75 bases roughly 25–40 div higher (≈200–350 chaos) for that “better” pool. The premium dwarfs the tiny per-hit difference — so buy the cheap high-ilvl base and pocket the gap. Bonus: top-tier resistance mods only roll at high item level, so if you also want a good res roll, ilvl 82 beats 75 there too.

The loop

01

Buy a cheap ilvl 75+ amulet base and get it to rare with a single mod.

02

Chaos-spam it — each orb rerolls one random mod from the pool.

03

Stop the instant you hit a jackpot: T1 Spirit or a +3 to a skill type.

04

Sell in Divine. The hit value dwarfs the chaos you spent → net-positive EV.

Why it's tiring, not hard: you must watch every single roll and never chaos over a mod that's already worth selling. That vigilance is the whole skill — the maths below is always in your favour.

Method & maths. Weights are the real 0.5 amulet pool (verified against Craft of Exile / poe2db): T1 Spirit = weight 400, each +3 skill = weight 100; the total pool grows from 163,800 (ilvl 75) to 173,650 (ilvl 82). Per-roll hit chance = ticked-mod weight ÷ total pool; expected chaos to hit = pool ÷ ticked weight; roll cost (div) = expected chaos ÷ your Divine:Chaos rate; expected win = spawn-weighted average of your ticked sale prices. EV per craft = expected win − base cost − roll cost. Amulet definitions and logs persist in your private D1 store; logged rows snapshot the prices at record time, so later edits to an amulet never rewrite past crafts.
